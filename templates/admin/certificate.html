Sign the CSR with your CA (outside ZIoCHub)

  1. Copy or download the CSR from step 1.
  2. Sign it with your internal CA (same process you used with OpenSSL before).
  3. Obtain three PEM files: server certificate, intermediate CA, and root CA.
  4. In step 3, paste each into its own field — ZIoCHub will build cert.pem as server → intermediate → root.

Paste the signed server certificate and CA chain. On save, all parts are combined into data/ssl/cert.pem.
