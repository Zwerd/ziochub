Downstream Systems (Distribution)

Register security products in your organization that consume IOCs from ZIoCHub via Feed, TAXII, or API push (Cortex XDR, Google SecOps, HTTP IOC push are tracked automatically when configured under Integrations). Match consumers by client IP on feed/TAXII pulls. Search & Investigate shows vendor icons when an IOC was likely distributed.

Vendor icons

Place official logos in static/img/vendors/ (production: /opt/ziochub/static/img/vendors/).

  • Pick a vendor from the list — icon preview appears below the dropdown.
  • Or check Custom vendor to enter a name and upload an icon (stored in static/img/vendors/custom/).
  • For catalog vendors you may also pre-place files as {vendor_id}.png under static/img/vendors/.
  • Formats: SVG, PNG, WebP, JPG (not .ico).
